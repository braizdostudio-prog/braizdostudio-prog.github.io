Privacy Policy – Warcarta

Last updated: May 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Warcarta ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is collected, used, and shared when you use the App.

2. Data Collection

The App does not require user registration and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email address, or phone number.

However, third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect certain data.

a) Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process the following data:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • App interactions (ad views, clicks)
  • Device and performance data

Purpose:

  • Advertising and marketing
  • Analytics
  • Fraud prevention

b) Diagnostics and Crash Data (Microsoft Application Insights)

The App uses Microsoft Application Insights to monitor performance and detect errors.

Data collected may include:

  • Crash logs and error reports
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Usage data (app performance, session activity)
  • IP address (may be anonymized)

Purpose:

  • Analytics
  • App functionality and stability

3. Data Sharing

Collected data may be shared with:

  • Google (AdMob – advertising, analytics)
  • Microsoft (Application Insights – analytics and diagnostics)

These third parties process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

Data is transmitted using secure encryption (HTTPS). Reasonable measures are implemented to protect collected data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above. Users may contact us to request deletion where applicable.

6. User Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing

To exercise your rights, contact us at: braizdo.studio@gmail.com

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us.

8. Consent (EEA Users)

For users in the EEA, a consent request may be displayed regarding personalized advertising, in compliance with GDPR.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated. Changes will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For any questions: braizdo.studio@gmail.com
